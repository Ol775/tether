# Fire TV setup

1. **Settings → My Fire TV → About**, then click your device name seven times to unlock Developer Options.
2. **Settings → My Fire TV → Developer Options → ADB debugging: On.** Turn on **Install unknown apps** too if you want to sideload.
3. **Settings → My Fire TV → About → Network** shows the IP address.
4. In Tether choose **Add device → Fire TV**, enter the IP (port 5555) and press Connect.
5. On the TV approve **Allow USB debugging?** and tick **Always allow**.

If the TV is not found, make sure the Mac and the TV are on the same network and that no VPN is intercepting local traffic.
